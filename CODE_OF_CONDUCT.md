
# TrustLens AI Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

TrustLens AI is an open-source initiative dedicated to advancing transparent, evidence-based, responsible, and trustworthy approaches to data and artificial intelligence assessment.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in the project.

This commitment applies regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, socioeconomic background, or other personal characteristics.

We welcome participation from software developers, researchers, students, data scientists, governance professionals, security specialists, organisations, and members of the wider open-source community.

Our community should encourage constructive collaboration, independent thinking, scientific integrity, and mutual respect.

## 1. Expected Behaviour

All community participants are expected to:

- Treat others with courtesy, dignity, and respect.
- Communicate professionally and constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives, experiences, and technical backgrounds.
- Provide helpful feedback supported by evidence where possible.
- Accept constructive criticism and respond professionally.
- Respect reasonable differences of opinion.
- Focus disagreements on technical issues, methods, evidence, and project objectives.
- Respect the privacy and confidentiality of others.
- Acknowledge contributions and intellectual ownership appropriately.
- Support an inclusive environment for new and experienced contributors.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work constructively towards solutions.
- Prioritise the safety and well-being of community members.

Professional disagreement is welcome when expressed respectfully and supported by appropriate reasoning.

## 2. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, bullying, or discrimination.
- Hate speech or derogatory comments targeting personal characteristics.
- Threats of violence or encouragement of harm.
- Personal attacks, insults, or repeated hostile communication.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or collaborative activities.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing confidential organisational information without authorisation.
- Repeated unwanted contact after a request to stop.
- Retaliation against individuals who raise concerns in good faith.
- Knowingly making false accusations to harm another participant.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional collaborative environment.

Disagreement with a technical proposal, research conclusion, or project decision is not itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## 3. Research Integrity and Responsible AI

Because TrustLens AI focuses on data readiness, governance, fairness, and operational trust, community members are expected to maintain high standards of research and technical integrity.

Contributors should:

- Present findings accurately and transparently.
- Avoid fabricating or deliberately misrepresenting results.
- Clearly distinguish experimental findings from validated functionality.
- Document important methodological assumptions.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and limitations.
- Avoid unsupported claims about fairness, safety, compliance, or trustworthiness.
- Respect intellectual property and appropriate attribution.
- Use datasets lawfully and responsibly.
- Avoid unnecessary exposure of sensitive information.
- Welcome constructive methodological review.

Assessment results should not be presented as independent proof that a dataset or AI system is fair, secure, trustworthy, legally compliant, or suitable for production deployment.

## 4. Respectful Technical Collaboration

Technical discussion and critical review are important parts of open-source development.

Contributors are encouraged to challenge assumptions, identify defects, propose alternatives, and question methodologies.

However, criticism should focus on:

- Code quality
- Technical correctness
- Research methodology
- Security and privacy
- Reliability and maintainability
- Testing and reproducibility
- User impact
- Project objectives

Feedback should avoid personal criticism, ridicule, or hostility.

Maintainers and contributors should aim to explain decisions clearly, particularly when rejecting proposals or requesting substantial revisions.

## 5. Inclusion and Accessibility

TrustLens AI welcomes participants with different levels of technical experience.

Community members should make reasonable efforts to:

- Use clear and understandable language.
- Explain unfamiliar technical concepts where appropriate.
- Avoid unnecessary gatekeeping.
- Support constructive questions.
- Consider accessibility in documentation and development.
- Encourage meaningful participation from underrepresented groups.
- Respect different communication styles and cultural backgrounds.

Participation should be based on the quality and relevance of contributions rather than personal status or background.

## 6. Privacy and Confidentiality

Community members must respect the privacy and confidentiality of others.

Participants should not:

- Publish private contact details without permission.
- Share credentials, access tokens, or confidential files.
- Disclose private vulnerability reports prematurely.
- Upload personal or sensitive datasets without appropriate authorisation.
- Use community participation to obtain confidential information improperly.

Security-sensitive concerns should be reported using the procedures in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## 7. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in TrustLens AI project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Code reviews
- GitHub discussions, where enabled
- Project documentation
- Community meetings and events
- Official project communication channels
- Other spaces where an individual is representing the TrustLens AI project

It may also apply to conduct outside official project spaces when that conduct directly and materially affects the safety or participation of project community members.

## 8. Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that may violate this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

Where available, use a private communication channel associated with the repository maintainer.

If no suitable private contact channel is published, you may request a private reporting method without disclosing sensitive details publicly.

Please do not include confidential personal information in a public GitHub issue.

A useful report may include:

- A description of the incident
- The approximate date and location of the incident
- Links to relevant discussions or messages
- Names or usernames of those involved, where appropriate
- Any immediate safety or confidentiality concerns
- Whether the behaviour is ongoing

Reports should be made in good faith.

Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be considered a violation of this policy.

## 9. Handling Reports

Project maintainers will aim to review reports fairly, respectfully, and without unnecessary delay.

Where appropriate, the review process may include:

1. Acknowledging receipt of the report.
2. Reviewing the information provided.
3. Requesting clarification where necessary.
4. Considering the context and seriousness of the behaviour.
5. Determining whether the Code of Conduct has been violated.
6. Taking proportionate action.
7. Communicating an outcome to relevant parties where appropriate.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is necessary to investigate concerns, protect individuals, or comply with legal obligations.

The project does not currently guarantee a fixed response or resolution timeframe.

## 10. Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for establishing and maintaining appropriate standards of conduct within project-managed spaces.

Maintainers may:

- Edit or remove inappropriate comments.
- Request changes to unacceptable behaviour.
- Close or restrict disruptive discussions.
- Decline or close contributions associated with serious misconduct.
- Restrict participation in project spaces.
- Apply temporary or permanent participation bans where justified.

Enforcement decisions should consider the seriousness of the conduct, surrounding circumstances, potential harm, and whether behaviour is repeated.

Where practical, actions should be proportionate, consistent, and documented appropriately.

## 11. Enforcement Guidelines

The following examples provide a general framework for responding to violations.

### Level 1 — Correction

**Examples:** Isolated unprofessional communication, inappropriate wording, or minor disrespectful behaviour.

**Possible response:**

- Private or public clarification
- Request to edit or remove content
- Reminder of expected conduct

### Level 2 — Formal Warning

**Examples:** Repeated inappropriate behaviour, targeted hostility, or continued misconduct after correction.

**Possible response:**

- Formal warning
- Required removal of offending content
- Notice that further violations may result in restrictions

### Level 3 — Temporary Restriction

**Examples:** Sustained harassment, significant disruption, or serious breaches of community expectations.

**Possible response:**

- Temporary restriction from discussions or contributions
- Limited participation privileges
- Conditions for future participation

### Level 4 — Permanent Restriction

**Examples:** Severe harassment, credible threats, serious privacy violations, or repeated misconduct despite earlier intervention.

**Possible response:**

- Permanent restriction from project-managed community spaces
- Removal of inappropriate contributions where necessary
- Additional protective action consistent with applicable platform policies

These examples are guidance rather than mandatory steps. Serious incidents may justify immediate protective action.

## 12. Fairness and Non-Retaliation

Reports should be assessed impartially.

Maintainers should avoid conflicts of interest where reasonably possible.

Participants should not face retaliation for:

- Reporting concerns in good faith
- Participating in an investigation
- Raising legitimate security or privacy issues
- Providing constructive technical criticism
- Challenging project decisions respectfully

Retaliation may result in additional enforcement action.

## 13. Maintainer Accountability

Maintainers are also subject to this Code of Conduct.

Maintainer status does not exempt an individual from community expectations.

Where a concern involves a maintainer, it should be reviewed by another appropriate maintainer where one is available.

If no independent maintainer is available, the project may need to seek an alternative impartial review arrangement.

- Community governance structures

This policy is informed by widely adopted open-source community conduct principles, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

It has been adapted to reflect the technical, research, privacy, and responsible AI considerations relevant to TrustLens AI.

## 17. Our Shared Responsibility

A successful open-source community depends on more than technical contributions.

It also requires respect, transparency, accountability, collaboration, and a willingness to learn from different perspectives.

By participating in TrustLens AI project spaces, contributors agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

Together, we aim to build a community that advances reliable, responsible, and evidence-based approaches to data and artificial intelligence assessment.
